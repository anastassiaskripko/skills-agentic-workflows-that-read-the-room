## Awesome Copilot workflow examples

The [github/awesome-copilot](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot) repository's `workflows/` folder showcases ready-to-use agentic workflows, including:

- `daily-issues-report.md` — automated daily summaries of repository issue activity.
- `ospo-contributors-report.md` and `ospo-org-health.md` — open source program office reporting on contributors and org health.
- `ospo-release-compliance-checker.md` and `ospo-stale-repos.md` — compliance checks and stale-repository triage.
- `relevance-check.md` and `relevance-summary.md` — filtering and summarizing incoming content for relevance.
- `weekly-comment-sync.md` — keeping cross-repository comments in sync on a weekly cadence.
